About

This website is designed to provide easy access to property information across the state of Idaho using high-resolution satellite imagery combined with accurate GIS boundary data.

Users can explore parcel boundaries, county boundaries, and the state boundary of Idaho on an interactive map. The platform allows searching by address, name, or company, saving properties of interest, and viewing key property details such as parcel ID, owner name, address, assessed value, and property taxes.

The goal is to offer a clear, modern, and efficient tool for anyone needing to view and research land and property information in Idaho.
