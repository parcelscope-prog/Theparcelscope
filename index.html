About Parcelscope

Parcelscope helps you explore public property information across Idaho, Washington, and Oregon.

Search by street address, owner / last name, city, or approximate parcel ID. Results include estimated acreage, assessed values, land use, and links to official county GIS portals where available.

Data Sources

  • Satellite imagery © Esri & partners
  • Geocoding via OpenStreetMap Nominatim
  • Boundaries derived from public Census / state GIS data
  • Parcel attributes are illustrative (real ownership data is published county-by-county and often requires direct assessor portals)

Disclaimer

This is a demonstration interface. Always verify ownership, acreage, and legal descriptions with the official county assessor or recorder. Parcelscope does not store or sell personal data beyond what you save locally in your browser.
